## Description

The Smoothstep filter extracts a range of grayscale values based on the minimum and maximum defined. Similar to Histogram Scan but interpolates for a smoother result.

It is used either directly on a fill layer or on a mask (black and white output) to isolate a grayscale range with a smoother transition than Threshold or Histogram Scan.
