# Log

![](../../assets/log.png)

The log is where Substance 3D Painter and [Plugins](../../features/plugins/plugins.md) can display status updates, keeping track of progress, warnings, and errors.

The log is divided into two tabs.

* The **General tab** shows information related to your project and scene, including export details.
* The **Assets tab** shows errors and logs specifically related to the **Assets panel**.
